# Legal & Contract Compliance Notes — NSW

**This is operational guidance, not legal advice.** Renovate 8 should have
its current contract reviewed by a solicitor or by the MBA NSW legal
support service. References are to publicly-available legislation and to
the standard MBA NSW residential building contract structure; the
specific clause numbers in the executed contract may differ.

## Governing instruments

1. **Home Building Act 1989 (NSW)** — primary legislation.
2. **Home Building Regulation 2014 (NSW)** — supporting regulations.
3. **Master Builders Association of NSW Residential Building Contract**
   — the form contract in use on the project (variants exist by year of
   issue and contract value; verify which form is in use on each job).
4. **NSW Fair Trading** — regulator. Builder license is issued and
   renewable through Fair Trading.

## Variations under the Home Building Act 1989 (NSW)

The variation provisions of the HBA require, in summary, that:

- **A variation to a residential building contract must be in writing**
  to be enforceable.
- The written variation must **describe the work** the variation covers
  and **state the change in the contract price** (or the method by which
  the price change will be calculated).
- The written variation should be **signed by, or on behalf of, each
  party** to the contract. Where signature is not practical at the time
  (e.g. emergency works), the variation must still be papered up
  promptly.
- The builder cannot recover for variation work unless these requirements
  are met (with limited exceptions — see HBA s.10 for the conditions
  under which a court may grant relief).

**System mitigation:** The Phase 1 / Phase 2 split exists specifically so
that the variation is in writing **immediately**, even if the final price
is not yet known.

## Variations under the MBA NSW residential contract

The MBA NSW form contract (across recent editions) requires:

- The owner may request a variation; the builder may also propose one.
- Variations must be documented on the contract's variation form (or an
  equivalent written notice).
- The builder must give written notice of:
  - the variation work to be performed,
  - the price (or method),
  - any extension of time required, and
  - the resulting effect on the contract sum.
- The owner must sign acceptance **before** the builder is required to
  perform the variation. If the owner does not sign within a reasonable
  time, the builder is not obliged to do the work.
- Limited exceptions exist for variations required by law, by an
  authority, or to address an emergency, where work can proceed before
  signature subject to prompt papering-up.

**System mitigation:** the Variation Notice template covers each required
element (description, price, time, contract sum effect, signature block,
emergency carve-out).

## Statutory warranties (HBA Schedule 2)

Variation work is still subject to the statutory warranties in HBA
Schedule 2 — workmanlike performance, fitness for purpose, materials new
and good quality, compliance with law, and completion within the agreed
time. The notice does **not** contract out of these (it cannot).

## Builder licensing

- Renovate 8 must hold a current **Contractor License** issued by NSW
  Fair Trading authorising the value and class of work.
- License number and (for individual licensees) contractor name **must
  appear on the variation notice**.
- If the contract value (original + variations) crosses a threshold that
  pushes the work into a higher license class, this is a compliance
  trigger — flag it on the register.

## Home Building Compensation Fund (insurance)

- Where the contract price exceeds the prescribed threshold (currently
  $20,000 inc GST for residential work in NSW), Home Building
  Compensation Cover is required **before** any work starts and **before
  any deposit is taken**.
- Variations that push a previously-exempt contract over the threshold
  trigger the insurance requirement retrospectively for the whole
  contract. Flag on the register.
- The certificate of insurance must be in the project file.

## GST

- Where Renovate 8 is GST-registered, prices on the variation notice
  must clearly show ex-GST, GST, and inc-GST. The template enforces
  this.
- Tax invoices for variation work must comply with the ATO tax invoice
  rules (supplier ABN, "tax invoice" wording, GST amount, etc.).

## What the variation notice MUST include

A NSW-compliant variation notice should contain, at minimum:

1. Builder name, business address, **ABN**, and **Contractor License
   number**.
2. Client (owner) name and the site address.
3. Reference to the head contract (date of contract, contract value).
4. Variation number, date issued, and version / status.
5. Clear description of the variation work (the "what").
6. Reason for the variation (the "why").
7. Price impact — ex-GST, GST, inc-GST. Either fixed, or the method of
   calculation (e.g. cost-plus a stated margin).
8. Time impact — days added or removed (zero is a valid answer; record
   it).
9. Updated contract sum showing original + previous variations + this
   variation = new total.
10. Acknowledgment / acceptance section, dated and signed by the owner.
11. Builder signature, dated.
12. Statutory notice that the work remains subject to the warranties in
    HBA Schedule 2.

The Word template covers all 12.

## What the notice MUST NOT include

- **Cost-to-me figures.** The notice goes to the client. Internal
  cost-to-me, margin %, subcontractor names with prices, etc. **stay in
  the register and the `02_Costing/` folder**, never on the notice.
- Misleading or unfair contract terms (Australian Consumer Law,
  schedule 2 to the Competition and Consumer Act).

## Disputes

- If a variation is disputed, the dispute resolution clauses of the head
  contract apply (typically: written notice of dispute → meeting →
  mediation → NCAT or court).
- NCAT (NSW Civil and Administrative Tribunal) has jurisdiction over
  home building disputes up to the prescribed monetary limit (currently
  $500,000 for residential building work claims under the HBA).
- The Phase 1 / Phase 2 paper trail, plus the signed acknowledgment, is
  the builder's main evidence in a dispute. **Keep it.**

## Records retention

- HBA statutory warranties run for **6 years** for major defects and **2
  years** for other defects, from the date of completion.
- Keep every variation file (folder + register + signed notice) for a
  minimum of **7 years** from practical completion. Dropbox retention
  must reflect this; do not auto-purge.
